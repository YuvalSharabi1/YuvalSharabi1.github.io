Privacy policy

Produpoint Facts uploader. Last updated November 8, 2026.

What this app is

The Produpoint Facts uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Produpoint Facts YouTube channel (@ProdupointFacts). It uploads the channel's own short videos to that channel through the YouTube API Services, and reads that channel's own video statistics. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Upload: one video for each new Produpoint Facts episode, with its title, description, the AI disclosure and the "not made for kids" setting (scope youtube.upload).
  • Read: the channel's own video list, view and comment counts, and subscriber count (scope youtube.readonly).

It does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, commenters or other channels.

What it keeps, and where

The YouTube video ID and the channel's own view and comment counts at 48 hours and 7 days, in the owner's private repository. The access token is stored only in the owner's private cloud environment. Nothing is sold or shared.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored data is deleted within 30 days of a request.
